# Billing and payments

## Ways to pay

- Mobile money: EcoCash and OneMoney, using merchant code 112233
- Bank transfer to our CBZ account, using your account number as the reference
- Debit order, which gives a 5% discount on the monthly fee of fibre plans
- Any Mhepo shop, in cash or by card

Mobile money payments reflect within 15 minutes. Bank transfers can take up to 2 working days to reflect.

## Due date and late payment

Fibre bills are due by the 7th of each month. If a bill is still unpaid on the 15th, the service is suspended. A reconnection fee of USD 5 applies when a suspended account is paid up, and service is restored within 2 hours of payment.

## Paying twice by mistake

If you pay the same bill twice, the second payment stays on your account as credit and reduces your next bill. If you would rather have the money back, ask for a refund within 30 days of the payment; refunds are paid to the original payment method within 10 working days.

## Statements

Monthly statements are emailed on the 1st and are also available in the Mhepo app under Billing > Statements.
