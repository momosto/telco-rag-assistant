# Mhepo Fibre home plans

Mhepo Fibre is our fibre-to-the-home service. All plans are uncapped and include a free Wi-Fi 6 router for as long as the contract runs.

## Plans and monthly prices

| Plan | Download / upload speed | Monthly price |
|------|-------------------------|---------------|
| Fibre Lite | 10 / 5 Mbps | USD 35 |
| Fibre Home | 25 / 10 Mbps | USD 55 |
| Fibre Plus | 50 / 25 Mbps | USD 85 |
| Fibre Max | 100 / 50 Mbps | USD 140 |

Prices include VAT. Plans are billed monthly in advance on the 1st of each month.

## Installation

Standard installation costs a once-off fee of USD 60. Installation is free on 12-month contracts for Fibre Plus and Fibre Max.

After the order is paid, a technician installs the service within 7 working days where fibre is already in the street. Where a new drop cable is needed from the street to the house, allow up to 21 working days.

## Contract terms

Fibre plans are offered month-to-month or on a 12-month contract. Month-to-month customers pay the installation fee even on Fibre Plus and Fibre Max.

## Upgrades and downgrades

You can upgrade at any time and the new speed is active within 24 hours. The price difference is pro-rated for the rest of the month. Downgrades take effect on the 1st of the following month.

## Moving house

We move your fibre service to a new address for a relocation fee of USD 30, provided fibre is available at the new address. Your contract continues; it does not restart.
