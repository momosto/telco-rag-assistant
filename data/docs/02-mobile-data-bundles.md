# Mobile data bundles

Data bundles are bought from the *300# USSD menu, the Mhepo app, or with mobile money.

## Daily, weekly and monthly bundles

| Bundle | Data | Validity | Price |
|--------|------|----------|-------|
| Daily 250 | 250 MB | 24 hours | USD 0.50 |
| Weekly 2 | 2 GB | 7 days | USD 3 |
| Monthly 10 | 10 GB | 30 days | USD 10 |
| Monthly 25 | 25 GB | 30 days | USD 20 |

## Night bundle

The Night bundle gives 5 GB for USD 1. It can only be used between 23:00 and 05:00 and expires at 05:00 on the morning after purchase.

## Social bundle

The Social bundle costs USD 2 and gives 1.5 GB for WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram only, valid for 7 days. Voice and video calls inside WhatsApp do use the Social bundle.

## Rollover

Unused data on Monthly 10 and Monthly 25 rolls over once, for 30 days, if you buy the same bundle again before the old one expires. Daily, weekly, Night and Social bundles do not roll over.

## Out-of-bundle rate

When you have no active bundle, data is charged at USD 0.10 per MB. You can block out-of-bundle data for free by dialling *300*9#.
