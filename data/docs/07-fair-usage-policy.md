# Fair usage policy

Uncapped fibre plans are subject to fair usage so that every customer gets good speeds at peak times.

The policy only applies to Fibre Lite and Fibre Home. Once a Fibre Lite or Fibre Home customer uses more than 1 TB in a calendar month, their speed may be reduced to 5 Mbps between 18:00 and 23:00 for the rest of that month.

Fibre Plus and Fibre Max are not subject to the fair usage policy.

Running a public Wi-Fi hotspot or reselling the connection breaks the terms of service on any home plan and can lead to disconnection.
