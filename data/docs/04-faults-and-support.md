## Before you report a fault

1. Check that the router's power light is on and the PON light is solid green.
2. If the LOS light is red, the fibre signal is lost; do not restart repeatedly, report the fault.
3. Restart the router once by switching it off for 30 seconds.

## Repair times

| Customer type | Target repair time |
|---------------|--------------------|
| Home fibre | 48 hours |
| Business fibre | 4 hours |

Every fault gets a reference number by SMS. Use it to track the fault in the app or on WhatsApp.

## Service credit

If a home fibre fault is not repaired within 72 hours, you receive a credit of one day's subscription for each extra day the service is down. The credit is applied automatically to your next bill.
