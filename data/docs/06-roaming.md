# International roaming

Roaming is switched off by default on prepaid lines. Activate it by dialling *300*7# before you travel.

## Roaming bundles

| Bundle | Countries | Includes | Validity | Price |
|--------|-----------|----------|----------|-------|
| SADC Roam 1 | South Africa, Botswana, Zambia, Mozambique | 1 GB data, 30 minutes | 7 days | USD 15 |
| SADC Roam 5 | South Africa, Botswana, Zambia, Mozambique | 5 GB data, 100 minutes | 30 days | USD 45 |

## Without a bundle

Outside a bundle, roaming data costs USD 1 per MB and calls cost USD 1.20 per minute, both made and received. Receiving SMS while roaming is free.
