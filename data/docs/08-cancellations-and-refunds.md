# Cancellations and refunds

## Cancelling a fibre service

Give 30 days of written notice, by email to support@mhepo.example or in a Mhepo shop. Month-to-month plans can be cancelled with no penalty after the notice period.

If you cancel a 12-month contract early, an early termination fee of 50% of the remaining monthly fees is charged.

## Returning the router

The router stays the property of Mhepo. Return it within 14 days of cancellation. A router that is not returned is charged at USD 80.

## Cooling-off period

New customers can cancel within 7 days of installation and get a full refund of the first month's fee and the installation fee, as long as the router is returned undamaged.

## Prepaid airtime

Prepaid airtime and bundles are not refundable and cannot be converted to cash.
