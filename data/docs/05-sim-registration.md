# SIM registration and replacement

## Registering a new SIM

Every SIM must be registered before it can be used. Bring your original national ID card or passport to a Mhepo shop. Photocopies and driver's licences are not accepted. Proof of residence is not required for a prepaid SIM.

A person may register at most 5 SIM cards on Mhepo in their name.

## Replacing a lost or stolen SIM

Report the loss on 0800 123 456 so the line is blocked straight away. A replacement SIM costs USD 2 and keeps your number, airtime and bundles. Replacements are only done in person at a Mhepo shop by the registered owner, with their original ID.

## eSIM

eSIMs are available for compatible phones at USD 3. Switching from a physical SIM to an eSIM keeps your number.
